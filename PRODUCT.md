## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small-community Minecraft server admins — often a friend group or a young, growing Discord community — who run (or want to run) a Cobblemon server and are choosing hosting. They are price-sensitive, non-technical-to-semi-technical, and have usually already been burned by lag, crashes, or unresponsive support on generic Minecraft hosts. The plan ladder (Poké Ball → Ultra Ball → Master Ball) also anticipates larger/semi-pro network operators (50+ players) as communities grow, but the entry-tier hobbyist admin is the primary buyer the site should speak to first.

## Product Purpose

Kium is Minecraft server hosting built specifically for Cobblemon communities: dedicated hardware, low-lag performance under Cobblemon's heavier TPS/dimension load, and real human support. Success is a community admin who sets up a server in minutes and stops thinking about infrastructure.

## Positioning

Configs and patches tuned specifically for Cobblemon's load profile (TPS under mob/dimension pressure), not a generic vanilla-Minecraft hosting template. Paired with real human support on Discord and hardware that isn't oversold (dedicated NVMe/CPU/RAM per plan, no shared-to-the-limit resources). This combination — Cobblemon-specific tuning + real hardware guarantees + human support — is the claim a generic Minecraft host could not truthfully copy without doing the same tuning work.

## Operating Context

Admins discover and evaluate Kium via the marketing site, then manage their relationship with Kium primarily through Discord (support, and likely order/setup). Servers run Cobblemon, a heavier mod than vanilla Minecraft, so mod compatibility and multi-dimension load are a factual part of the hosting workload, not a marketing detail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three hosting tiers by player count and dedicated resources: Poké Ball (1–15 players, 4GB RAM / 2 vCPU), Ultra Ball (15–50 players, 10GB RAM / 4 vCPU, marked "Más Popular"), Master Ball (50+ players, 24GB RAM / 8 dedicated vCPU, custom pricing).
- Support channel is Discord; no ticket system is described.
- Copy and UI are in Spanish (es-AR register — informal, direct, e.g. "nos cansamos del lag... con la misma bronca").
- Undecided: actual monthly pricing for Poké Ball and Ultra Ball (currently placeholder "Desde $X / mes"); Master Ball is intentionally custom-quote, not a placeholder.
- Undecided: real Discord/X/YouTube URLs (currently "#" placeholders in the footer).

## Product Principles

1. Cobblemon-specific, not generic Minecraft — every technical and marketing claim should trace back to Cobblemon's actual demands (mod load, dimensions, TPS), not boilerplate hosting copy.
2. Speak to the hobbyist/small-community admin first — the entry tier and its anxieties (lag, being ignored by support, hidden costs) are the primary persuasion target, even as the plan ladder scales up.
3. Real hardware, real people, real price — "no promesas" (no empty promises) is a stated brand value; avoid copy or design that reads as inflated or evasive about limits/pricing.
4. Placeholder honesty — until real numbers/testimonials/pricing exist, treat the site as pre-launch and do not let design work quietly convert placeholders into implied facts.
